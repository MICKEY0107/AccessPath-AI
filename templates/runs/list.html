Evaluate a real website

Add a journey on a live public website. A live planner moves through it with the keyboard only, and the run records the same evidence as the reference journeys.

For safety these runs are read-only. They never submit forms or send data, and they never open popups, downloads, or other websites. Searching, browsing, and finding a page work; signing in or checking out stops safely at the first submit.
